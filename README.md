## 🎚 Virtual soundcheck — ZOOM L-20 / L-20R — new in 2.9.2

**Soundcheck without the band.**  Record the band once on the mixer's SD card, then play the tracks back through
their own channels and set up the mix in peace: EQ, faders, monitor sends, even the AI sound check — the day
before the gig or with the venue still empty.

- **One button for everything**: AutoFonic Mixer arms the channels, records, rewinds and plays, in **loop**: when
  the recording ends it starts again
- the recorded tracks of **CH1–16** come back into their channels, exactly as the mixer's REC/PLAY keys do
- **"Back to live"** puts every channel back as it was, also if you close the program, the Bluetooth drops or the
  demo ends: no channel stays without its input at the gig
- **PC and Android**, **in the free demo too**

Only for the **ZOOM LiveTrak L-20 and L-20R**: they are the only LiveTrak mixers whose tracks can be switched to
PLAY one by one from an app.  First version: your reports from real gigs are welcome.

## 📱 Monitors from the phone — new in 2.9.2

**Every musician sets their own monitor mix, from their own phone.**  AutoFonic Mixer on the PC holds the mixer;
the musicians scan a QR code and a page opens in the browser: **iPhone, iPad and Android, nothing to install**.

- each one picks their monitor (name them on the PC, e.g. *C · Drummer*) and moves only **their own** mix: how much
  of each channel they hear, plus the monitor volume
- **several phones at once**; what the sound engineer changes shows up on the phones by itself
- **safe**: a 4-digit code, only the monitor sends (never the main mix, mutes or scenes), only from the local network
- ZOOM L12next, L-20 / L-20R, L6 and Behringer X Air / X32.  On the PC: **☰ More → 📱 Monitors from the phone**
- free during the 14-day PC demo

**Connecting the phones:** PC and phones on the same Wi-Fi, no internet needed.  The safest is any Wi-Fi router on
stage; the venue's Wi-Fi works too (not the "guest" network), or a phone's or the PC's hotspot.  If Windows asks
for network access, answer *Allow*; if it works at home but not at the venue, set that Wi-Fi as a *private* network.

## 💻 On the PC (Windows · Linux · Mac)

- **The whole mixer on screen**: faders, mute, solo, phase, pan, meters, monitor sends, scenes, recorder.
- **Channel page** 🆕: EQ curve, fader with meter, values in dB and Hz (click the channel name).
- **Full-screen sound check** in 4 phases: room silence, one instrument at a time (gain, sound, drum phase),
  the whole band (faders get balanced), saved to the scene you choose.
- **AI Studio / Live**: *Assist* suggests, *Auto* corrects the faders while you play, with the right rules for
  each instrument.  An optional room mic also listens to the room.
- **SD export**: the recordings on the SD card become WAV tracks named after the channel, plus the master and
  a stereo mix, in WAV or MP3.
- **Tuner** for guitar, bass and drums.
- **Respects your mixer**: never changes scene by itself, and the AI never touches monitor sends or master.

Sound check and AI need the **USB cable** (it carries every channel's audio) and, on L12next and L-20, the **BTA-1**.

<summary><b>📖 Installation and tips</b></summary>

**Windows**
1. Install your mixer's **ZOOM driver** (from the ZOOM website): it is needed for the audio of every channel.
2. Run the downloaded `AutoFonicMixer-DEMO-windows-x64-setup.exe` file.  If SmartScreen warns you: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
3. Switch on the PC's Bluetooth (don't pair the BTA-1: the program finds it by itself) and plug in the USB cable.

**Linux**
1. Unpack the `.tar.gz`, run `sh installa.sh` in the folder, log out and back in.
2. Start "AutoFonic Mixer" from the menu: USB, audio and Bluetooth are found automatically.

**Mac**: open the `.dmg`, drag the app into Applications; the first time: right click → Open.  The Mac version

L-20, plug the BTA-1 in **before** switching the mixer on and close the ZOOM app (the BTA-1 accepts one
connection only).  With the L6: USB-C cable into the mixer's USB port, or a CME WIDI Jack in the MIDI socket.

**Mixer in another room**: Bluetooth has a short range.  Better: a small computer next to the mixer on the USB
cable, controlled from the other room with remote desktop over Wi-Fi.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is there a ZOOM L-20 / L-20R app for Android?**  Yes: AutoFonic Mixer, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.autofonic.mixer).
ZOOM's L-20 Control app exists only for iOS.  It connects with the ZOOM BTA-1 Bluetooth adapter, like ZOOM's app.

**Can I control a ZOOM LiveTrak L-20R without an iPad?**  Yes, from an Android phone or from a Windows, Linux or
Mac computer: faders, mute, solo, pan, EQ, monitor mixes A–F, effect returns, scenes and the SD recorder.

**Is there an app for the ZOOM L12next / L-12 on Android or Windows?**  Yes, AutoFonic Mixer supports the L12next
on Android and on the PC, where it also does the sound check and the automatic mix.  The original L-12 is not
supported.

**Is there an iPhone / iPad app?**  Not yet.  But with the [monitors from the phone](#phone-monitors) every
musician can set their own monitor mix from an iPhone or iPad browser, with AutoFonic Mixer on the PC holding
the mixer.

**Does it work with the ZOOM L6 / L6max?**  Yes, with the USB-C cable, or wireless with a CME WIDI Jack.

**Does it work with the Behringer XR18, XR12, XR16 or X32 / Midas M32?**  Yes, on the PC
(Windows, Linux, Mac) over the network: faders, mute, EQ, compressor, gate, sends, mute groups, scenes.  With
the XR18 / MR18 and the X32 (X-USB card) also the sound check and the automatic mix, listening to the USB audio.

**Is it free?**  The PC version is a free demo; on Android there is a 7-day free trial, then a one-time purchase.

## 🎚 Soundcheck virtuale — ZOOM L-20 / L-20R — novità della 2.9.2

**Il soundcheck senza la band.**  Registri la band una volta sulla scheda SD del mixer, poi rimandi le tracce
nei loro canali e sistemi il mix con calma: EQ, fader, mandate ai monitor, anche il sound check con l'AI — il
giorno prima del concerto o con il locale ancora vuoto.

- **Un pulsante per tutto**: AutoFonic Mixer prepara i canali, registra, torna all'inizio e fa ripartire, in
  **loop**: quando la registrazione finisce riparte da capo
- le tracce registrate di **CH1–16** tornano nei loro canali, proprio come con i tasti REC/PLAY del mixer

## ❓ Domande frequenti

**Esiste un'app per ZOOM L-20 / L-20R su Android?**  Sì: AutoFonic Mixer, su [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.autofonic.mixer).
L'app L-20 Control di ZOOM esiste solo per iOS.  Si collega con l'adattatore Bluetooth ZOOM BTA-1, come l'app ZOOM.

**Posso comandare un ZOOM LiveTrak L-20R senza iPad?**  Sì, dal telefono Android o da un computer Windows, Linux
o Mac: fader, mute, solo, pan, EQ, mix monitor A–F, ritorni effetti, scene e registratore SD.

**C'è un'app per ZOOM L12next / L-12 su Android o Windows?**  Sì, AutoFonic Mixer supporta il L12next su Android
e sul PC, dove fa anche il sound check e il mix automatico.  Il vecchio L-12 non è supportato.

**C'è un'app per iPhone / iPad?**  Non ancora.  Ma con i [monitor dal telefono](#monitor-dal-telefono) ogni
musicista regola il suo monitor dal browser di un iPhone o di un iPad, con AutoFonic Mixer sul PC che tiene il
mixer.

**Funziona con ZOOM L6 / L6max?**  Sì, con il cavo USB-C, oppure senza fili con un CME WIDI Jack.

**Funziona con Behringer XR18, XR12, XR16 o X32 / Midas M32?**  Sì, sul PC (Windows, Linux,
Mac) in rete: fader, mute, EQ, compressore, gate, mandate, mute group, scene.  Con XR18 / MR18 e X32 (scheda
X-USB) anche il sound check e il mix automatico, ascoltando l'audio USB.

**È gratis?**  La versione per PC è una demo gratuita; su Android ci sono 7 giorni di prova gratis, poi un solo
acquisto.
